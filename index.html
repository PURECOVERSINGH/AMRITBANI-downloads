Install and listen

  1. Android: Open the downloaded APK. Allow installation from that specific browser/file manager if prompted, then turn that permission off afterward.
  2. TV: Transfer the TV APK with USB or a trusted file-transfer method, install it, and open AMRITBANI from Apps. Use your remote's directional pad and OK button.
  3. iPhone/iPad: Sign the corrected IPA on your computer with your own Apple ID. After installation, search AMRITBANI in App Library and trust the developer profile in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management if prompted. For immediate use, open the web player in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen.

Previously installed our original TV debug build? Uninstall it before installing this release-signed version.
